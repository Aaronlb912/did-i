Did I

A habit tracker widget you can answer from

The Did I widget shows the next open question on your home screen. Tap Did or Not yet on the widget itself. The app does not need to open.

Get Did I on the App StoreJoin the Android beta

See it, answer it

If it is on your home screen, you see it every time you unlock your phone. That is the whole point: the reminder sits where your eyes already go.

Always the next one

Answer one and the widget moves to the next question. When you are done for the day it says so.

Sorted by folder

Keep questions in folders like Morning, Me or Home, and they come up in order.

Questions

Can I answer without opening the app?

Yes. Tap Did or Not yet on the widget.

Does the widget work on Android?

Yes. Android is in beta on Google Play.
