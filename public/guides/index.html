Guides

How Did I fits the way you already use your phone.

  • How to remember if you did something today
    Did I drink water? Did I stretch? Why you forget doing routine things, and a simple way to know for sure: ask once, answer Did or Not yet, and check later.
  • A habit tracker for people who forget to open habit trackers
    You download a habit tracker, use it for three days, then forget it exists. Did I flips that: the question comes to your widget, watch or a reminder.
  • How to build a habit with one question a day
    Big habit plans fall apart. One small yes or no question a day is easier to keep. How to pick the question, when to ask it, and what to do on a miss.
  • How to ask someone if they did a chore without nagging
    Texting "did you take the trash out?" again and again turns into nagging. Ask once instead: send a question that waits in their inbox until they answer.
  • Streaks without the guilt: what Not yet is for
    Streaks help until one breaks and you quit. Did I uses Did and Not yet, lets you tap Did later that day, and has a gentle mode that hides the number.
  • A habit tracker for ADHD that you do not have to remember to open
    Did I is a free habit tracker for ADHD brains. The question shows up on your widget, watch or a reminder, one at a time. Swipe Did or Not yet. No guilt for a miss.
  • A habit tracker widget you can answer from
    Did I puts your next habit question in a home screen widget. Tap Did or Not yet right on the widget. Free habit tracker for iPhone, Android beta.
  • An Apple Watch habit tracker that asks you
    Answer your habits from your Apple Watch. Did I asks one question at a time, a ring fills as you go, and your buddy comes along. Free on iPhone.
  • A family habit tracker that checks in without nagging
    Start a family in Did I and share a code. "Did you take the dog out?" shows up for everyone. Keep a streak together and cheer when someone finishes.
  • A habit reminder app that asks, not nags
    Did I is a habit reminder app that asks one question at the time you set. Swipe Did or Not yet. Free on iPhone, Android in beta.
  • A daily checklist app that shows one thing at a time
    A daily checklist app that shows one item at a time so you always know what is next. Did I is free on iPhone, with widgets and Apple Watch.
  • A free habit tracker app with streaks and small rewards
    Did I is a free habit tracker app with streaks, coins, a shop of buddies and looks, widgets and Apple Watch. iPhone now, Android in beta.
