How to ask someone if they did a chore without nagging

You text "did you take the trash out?", then "??", then "hello?". Nobody likes that thread, on either side. There is a calmer way to check on shared chores.

Why reminders turn into nagging

Each extra text feels like pressure, and each missed text feels like being ignored. The chore gets tangled up with the relationship, and both get worse.

Ask once, in one place

Instead of a thread, send one question that waits until they are ready. They see it when they check, and you do not have to keep asking.

Agree on the question together

Decide the chore and the day out loud first: trash goes out Tuesday night. Then the question is a shared plan, not a surprise demand.

Do the chore together

Some chores belong to more than one person. Answer them together, see who has done today, and keep a streak as a household.

How Did I does it

With Did you, you send a friend or family member a question, once or every day. It lands in their inbox and they choose to keep it. With Did we, you pick who is in and keep a streak together. Family questions show up for everyone in the family.

Say thanks

When someone finishes, send a cheer. A small thank you does more for the next time than any reminder.

Questions

Do I still have to text them?

No. The question waits in their inbox in Did I, so there is no text thread to keep up.

Can we share a streak?

Yes. Did we questions keep a streak for everyone who is in.

Is it free?

Yes. Did I is free on iPhone, and Android is in beta.
