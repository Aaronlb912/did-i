## The job

A person installs Did I. That night they have a question
and a reason to hit DID. The next night they open it again
because the board remembers they came.

The win is **tonight is done**. Not XP. Not a shop.
Not a character.

---

## How the game works (the object)

The object is a question board.

- Each night you **show up** (open the app). That is a mark
  even before a tap.
- Each due question you answer (DID, late, or nope) is a
  tap. Nope is honest. It does not smash the rest.
- When every due question is answered, **tonight is done**.
  That night adds a **streak**. Streak is 0 to 5. Miss a due
  night and it drops to 0 the next count. No guilt screen.
- Closed-list **marks** unlock once. Named. Not “Level 12”.

Why open tomorrow: the streak is visible on Home. After tonight
is done, the screen says come back tomorrow. The phone can
ping at ask-after.

count, last marks, last time, week row. No directory of
every page.

quietMode hides the tray, not the unlocks.
