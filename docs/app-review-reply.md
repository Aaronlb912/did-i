# Reply to App Review: Guideline 2.1, Information Needed (1.3.1, Oct 2026)

Apple asked for 7 things. Paste the text below as the reply in App Store Connect
(App Review > the submission > Reply to App Review), attach the screen recording,
and also add it to App Review Information > Notes.

---

Hello, and thank you for reviewing Did I.

**1. Screen recording**
Attached: a recording on an iPhone running the latest iOS. It starts at launch and shows:
- The first screen: Sign in with Apple, and the reviewer sign-in (tap "Reviewer sign-in" at the bottom of the first screen).
- Adding a question, then answering it on Tonight by swiping right (Did) or left (Not yet).
- The home screen widget.
- Friends and family: adding a friend by friend ID, and Report and Block from a friend's row.
- The Shop: buying gems with In-App Purchase, and spending gems on a look.
- Account deletion: Account > Delete my account > Delete it.

**2. Purpose and audience**
Did I is for people who forget to open habit trackers. It brings the question to you, on a widget, a watch or a reminder at the time you set, for the small things people forget they did, such as "Did I take my meds?", "Did I text them back?" or "Did I drink enough water?". It asks one question at a time and you swipe to answer. It is for adults and teens who second-guess themselves. The value is knowing for sure that you did something, without a long to-do list.

**3. How to use it**
- Sign in: the app opens on a welcome screen. Tap "Reviewer sign-in" at the bottom. An email and password form opens. The email and password are in the Notes field. You can also use the app without an account.
- The review account already has sample questions, a month of answers, a friend and a family.
- Tonight: swipe right for Did, left for Not yet.
- Questions tab: add, edit or remove questions.
- Menu: Friends and family, Shop, Account and Settings.

**4. External services**
- Supabase: database and sign-in (stores accounts, questions, answers and friends).
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: login.
- Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit): gems.
- Apple Push Notification service: reminders and friend notifications.
- Expo Updates: delivers JavaScript updates to the app.
No AI services and no advertising or tracking SDKs.

**5. Regions**
The app works the same in every region. It is translated into 9 languages, and the features are the same in all of them.

**6. Regulated industry or third-party material**
Not applicable. Did I is not a medical app and gives no medical advice. Questions are written by the user. It contains no licensed third-party content.

**7. In-App Purchase**
Gems are consumable purchases in packs of 1, 3, 6, 14, 40 and 80. Users spend them on cosmetic items: color looks for question cards, cheer animations, small buddy characters, and gifts for friends. Nothing in the app requires gems. To buy: open the Menu, tap Shop, then choose a gem pack.

Thank you.
