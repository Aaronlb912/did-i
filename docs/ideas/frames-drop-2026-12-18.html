Frame drop · 2026-12-18

Gingerbread, Snowman, Solstice

Three new frames: a still gingerbread border for coins, a snowman you build as you answer for gems, and the longest night for the solstice. Each sits around the card and never on the text. Switch theme, look and motion to check them.

Port notes: every new drawing uses only Path, Circle, Ellipse, Rect, Line, LinearGradient, RadialGradient and ClipPath, in card space like art.jsx (0,0 is the card's top-left; art reaches at most 24 px out). Motion here is CSS; in the app it becomes Mover worklets on the frame clock and stops for still tiles, reduced motion and low power.
