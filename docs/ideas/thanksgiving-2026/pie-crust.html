Pie Crust

A special frame, free Nov 23 to 26 and kept after. Your card sits in a crimped pie crust. It starts as pale dough and bakes a little more golden with every Did today. When today is done, steam rises from the top. Switch theme, look and motion to check it.

Port notes: the crust uses only Path, Ellipse, Circle, LinearGradient and RadialGradient, in card space like art.jsx. The oven glow sits under the card; crust, leaves and steam sit outside it. Motion here is CSS; in the app it becomes Mover worklets and stops for still tiles, reduced motion and low power.
