Did I · mascot character sheet

Meet Iffy.

A little question mark who is never quite sure. Swipe Did, and Iffy stands up straight into a check. Iffy is the feeling of "did I?" turning into "I did", in one character.

The name

Iffy: "not sure", said in a friendly way. That is exactly who he is before you swipe. Short, playful, never mocks the user, and it is not a person's name. Before using it in the App Store or ads, run a quick trademark search (the USPTO search tool) to be safe.

How Iffy is built

Two shapes and two big eyes. No mouth: the body shows the mood. Simple enough to draw in five seconds, to read at 16 pixels, and to animate from a question mark into a check.

ConstructionOne thick stroke (the hook) plus the dot. Stroke = 18% of the height. Ends round. The face lives on its own anchor.
Proportions
  • Height: 1 unit = the hook plus the dot.
  • Stroke: 0.18 units, never thinner: the face must fit inside it.
  • Face anchor: the top of the hook on the ?, the long arm on the ✓. The face turns with the stroke, so it always sits inside it.
  • Eyes: big and white, 0.08 units across, nearly touching. Pupils show where Iffy looks; closed arcs show happy or asleep.
  • Body language: the ? curls up small when worried, stretches tall when curious, hops when surprised, droops for Not yet. The ✓ stands taller when proud and lies down to sleep.
  • Dot: 0.21 units, sits just below the hook. The dot is Iffy's foot: it bounces when Iffy walks.
  • No arms, no legs, no outline. Iffy acts with lean, eyes and the dot.

From "did I?" to "I did"

Iffy's signature move. The top of the hook swings out, the curve straightens, the dot joins the stroke, and Iffy stands up into a check. Used for the first swipe, a cleared night and the cheers.

Expressions

Only the eyes change on the face; the body does the rest. Eight moods cover everything the app, the widget and videos need.

Poses

On light and in small sizes

Personality and voice

Who Iffy is
  • Forgetful too. Iffy gets it. Iffy is never the know-it-all.
  • Gentle. Never shames, never guilt-trips, never says "you failed".
  • Quietly proud. Celebrates small: a check, a sparkle, a nod.
  • Sleepy at night. Once you're done, Iffy wants you to sleep, not scroll.
  • Plain-spoken. Short sentences, no hype, no em dashes, same rules as the app.
Sounds like
Did you? No rush. Swipe when you know.
All clear. Go to bed.
Not yet is fine. It can still turn into Did.
Seven nights. Iffy is standing very straight.
No questions yet. What do you always forget?
Never sounds like

"You missed your pills again."

"Don't break your streak!"

"Iffy is sad you didn't come back."

"Crush your goals tonight!"

Iffy can be playful, never pushy. The opposite of a guilt-trip owl.

Where Iffy lives

In the app
  • App icon: Iffy's face on the question mark.
  • First open: the opening swipe ends with Iffy standing up into a check.
  • Empty states: curious Iffy, "What do you always forget?"
  • All clear: sleeping Iffy on Home and the widget.
  • Cheers: a Did can end with a tiny Iffy hop.
  • Notifications: Iffy's face as the small icon.
Outside the app
  • Short videos: Iffy in the corner reacting (unsure, then a check).
  • Store screenshots and the website.
  • Stickers: the eight faces as a sticker pack (iMessage, WhatsApp).
  • Merch later: a pin of the ? and the ✓.
Iffy and the buddies

Iffy is the face of Did I, not a buddy you buy. The buddies (cat, frog, owl and the rest) stay as collectibles. Iffy can appear next to your buddy in cheers, but never wears accessories or looks: Iffy always looks like Iffy, so people recognize it everywhere.

Rules

Do
  • Keep the stroke thick and the ends round.
  • Leave clear space around Iffy of at least the dot's size.
  • Use one Iffy per screen.
  • Let Iffy react to the user, never the other way round.
  • Respect reduced motion: Iffy stays still.
Don't
  • Add arms, legs, clothes or an outline.
  • Stretch, squash or recolor Iffy outside the look accent.
  • Make Iffy sad, angry or disappointed at the user.
  • Put Iffy next to medical claims or health advice.
  • Use Iffy to push purchases.
