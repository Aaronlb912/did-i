Frame drop · 2027-01-29

Lace, Paper Boats, Thaw

Three new frames: a paper lace doily for coins, a living harbor of paper boats for gems, and a late winter thaw that is free from February 20 to March 5. Each sits around the card and never on the text. Switch theme, look and motion to check them.

Port notes: every new drawing uses only Path, Circle, Ellipse, Rect, Line, LinearGradient, RadialGradient and ClipPath, in card space like art.jsx (0,0 is the card's top-left; art reaches at most 20 px out at the sides and bottom and 8 px at the top). Motion here is CSS; in the app it becomes Mover worklets on the frame clock and stops for still tiles, reduced motion and low power.
