20 new frames

Seven coin frames that stay still, seven gem frames that move, and six specials that come with a season or get earned. None of them repeats a frame already in the app. All art sits in the gap outside the card, so the question is never covered. Pick the ones to build, and each drop can take one of each kind.

Coin · still · 60 to 120 coins

Coin frames

Drawn once, no motion. Priced like the coin frames already in the shop.

Gem · animated · 3 gems

Gem frames

Each one is a little scene: a band of material with a glow, motion inside it, and set pieces like planets, clouds, fish or volcanoes. Motion stops for reduced motion and low power, like the gem frames already in the shop.

Special · seasonal or earned

Specials

Never sold. Seasonal ones come back each year for their dates; earned ones stay once you have them.

To build one: art goes in native/components/frames/art.jsx, the id in geometry.js and frames.js, and its name in the 9 locale files. Gem frames use the same clock and particles as the ones there now.
