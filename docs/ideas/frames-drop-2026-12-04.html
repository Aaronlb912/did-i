Cable Knit, Winter Window, Candlelight

Three winter frames: a still cream sweater for coins, a living frosty window for gems, and candles you earn with a 14-night streak. Each sits around the card and never on the text. Switch theme, look and motion to check them.

Port notes: every new drawing uses only Path, Circle, Ellipse, Rect, Line, LinearGradient, RadialGradient and ClipPath, in card space like art.jsx (0,0 is the card's top-left). Uniform strokes are batched into one Path each. Motion here is CSS; in the app it becomes Mover worklets on the frame clock and stops for still tiles, reduced motion and low power.
