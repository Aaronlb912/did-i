Frames that live your night

Each of these reacts to something only Did I has: tonight's questions, your swipes, your streak, your buddy, the evening itself. Move the controls and watch them change.

How they would work in the app: tonight's answered count, the swipe position (already a shared value on the swipe card), the streak and the clock are all known on Home. Each frame reads them and redraws only what changed. Motion stops for reduced motion and low power like today's gem frames.
