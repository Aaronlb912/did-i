Pumpkin Patch

A special frame, free Oct 28 to 31 and kept after. One jack-o'-lantern for every question today. Each Did lights one from inside. Switch theme, look and motion to check it.

Port notes: pumpkin drawing uses only Path, Ellipse, Circle, Line, LinearGradient, RadialGradient and ClipPath, in card space like art.jsx. Glow and moon are under the card; vine and pumpkins sit just outside it. Motion here is CSS; in the app it becomes Mover worklets and stops for still tiles, reduced motion and low power.
