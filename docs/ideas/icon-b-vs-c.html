App icon · B vs C

Same icon, two Iffys

Both keep your three hand-drawn question marks, with Iffy's eyes on the middle one. The difference is how Iffy looks inside the app.

Option B

Glossy Iffy inside the app

The icon stays hand-drawn; inside the app Iffy is the shaded, round character. More presence and personality on big screens; the icon and the in-app Iffy look related but not identical.

Option C

Hand-drawn Iffy everywhere

Iffy is drawn with the same thin lime marker as the icon, everywhere. One consistent, handmade style from the home screen to the last screen. Softer and quieter; less pop at large sizes.
